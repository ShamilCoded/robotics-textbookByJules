# Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

**Focus:** Physics simulation and environment building.

Simulation is crucial for training robots safely before deploying them in the real world.

*   **Simulating physics, gravity, and collisions in Gazebo**: Creating realistic physical interactions.
*   **High-fidelity rendering and human-robot interaction in Unity**: Visualizing the robot in high quality.
*   **Simulating sensors**:
    *   LiDAR
    *   Depth Cameras
    *   IMUs
